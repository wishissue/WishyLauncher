# wishy-home theme credits

This theme contains no third-party artwork, logos or trademarks. The look is original: the same colours as the launcher's Settings, flat surfaces and Plus Jakarta Sans.

## Fonts

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold)** (`assets/fonts/PlusJakartaSans-*.ttf`) by The Plus Jakarta Sans Project Authors. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: https://github.com/tokotype/PlusJakartaSans. Full text: `licenses/OFL-PlusJakartaSans.txt` in the Wishy Launcher repository.

## Images

None. The background is a flat colour from the colour scheme; the person's own wallpaper from Settings replaces it. Apps without a banner show their icon on a plain tile.
